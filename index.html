Before you sleep

A web page can only listen while the phone stays unlocked and the page stays in front. The app keeps the screen on and turns it black after 20 seconds, but these steps make sure nothing interrupts it.

  1. Plug in the charger. The screen stays on all night.
  2. Turn Low Power Mode off. It locks the screen after 30 seconds, which stops the recording.
  3. Turn the brightness down and switch on Do Not Disturb or Sleep focus, so calls and messages don’t interrupt.
  4. Tap Start, then put the phone down within 1–2 m of your head, microphone uncovered. Don’t press the side button and don’t switch apps.
  5. If the phone locks anyway, set Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never for the night.
  6. In the morning, tap the black screen, then tap Stop.

Listens all night

Measures your room’s background noise, then reacts to every sound that rises above it.

Keeps only snore clips

A snore is a dull, low-pitched burst of ¼ to 4 seconds that repeats with your breathing. Talking, knocking, traffic and coughs are discarded. Only short clips that sound like snoring are kept; they can include sounds right around a snore, and the detection can be wrong.

Snorewatch estimates snoring from sound alone. It is not a medical device and cannot detect sleep apnea. If you stop breathing at night, wake up gasping or feel very tired during the day, talk to a doctor.

Privacy: audio is analyzed on this device and never uploaded. Sounds that are not snores are discarded within a few seconds; only snore clips stay in memory until you close the page.
